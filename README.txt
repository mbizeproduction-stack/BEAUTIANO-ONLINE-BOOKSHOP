BEAUTIANO ONLINE BOOKSHOP WEBSITE
=================================

CONTENTS
- index.html: Homepage
- about.html: About the shop
- shop.html: Product catalogue with search and category filter
- cart.html: Shopping cart demo
- quotes.html: Quote request form
- contacts.html: Contact and order enquiry page
- styles.css: Website styling and responsive layout
- app.js: Product display, search, cart and interactions
- assets/: Logo, banner and product illustrations (SVG)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern web browser.
3. Use the navigation to browse the pages.
4. Add products to the cart; the demo cart is saved in your browser.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE
This is a front-end website prototype. It does not yet process real payments,
send form submissions, manage stock, or calculate delivery. To launch as a
real online shop, connect a secure backend, payment provider, email/order
handling, product inventory and verified business contact details.

Brand colours: navy, bright pink, white and soft light backgrounds.
Tagline: Shop Smart. Study Better.
